# Catastrophe Risk & Insurance Claims Analytics Dashboard

**Context:**
This project simulates a real-world use case for an insurance/risk analytics company. The goal is to analyze natural disaster data (floods, earthquakes, storms) and insurance claims to identify high-risk regions, claim patterns, and financial impact. It reflects how enterprise companies assess risk exposure and make data-driven business decisions.

### 3. RISK MODEL

**Risk Scoring Logic:**
The model calculates a composite "Risk Score" out of 100 by weighting three key factors:
1. **Severity (40%):** The physical intensity of the disaster (e.g., Richter scale, category).
2. **Frequency (30%):** Historical probability and volume of claims generated by the event.
3. **Financial Impact (30%):** The total dollar amount of claims submitted.

**Categorization:**
- **High Risk (Score 70-100):** Immediate premium adjustments and strict underwriting required.
- **Medium Risk (Score 40-69):** Standard monitoring; localized reinsurance may be needed.
- **Low Risk (Score 0-39):** Standard coverage policies apply.

### 4. KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS

1. **North America & Asia-Pacific are the Highest Risk Zones:** Together, these regions account for over 65% of total financial losses, primarily driven by Hurricanes and Floods respectively.
2. **Earthquakes Have the Highest Severity-to-Cost Ratio:** While occurring less frequently than floods, single earthquake events generate the highest average payout per claim.
3. **Upward Trend in Financial Losses:** Insurance claim volumes have steadily increased by an average of 8-12% YoY since 2018, indicating a rising baseline of catastrophe risk.
4. **Flood Claims are High-Frequency, Low-Severity:** Floods generate the highest number of individual claims (high operational burden) but generally have lower per-claim payouts compared to wildfires or earthquakes.
5. **Concentrated Exposure in High-Risk Segments:** 25% of the geographic portfolio falls into the "High Risk" tier, but these areas are responsible for over 50% of the total claims payout.

### 5. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Dynamic Premium Pricing:** Implement dynamic, geo-spatial pricing models for property insurance in North America and Asia-Pacific to offset the rising YoY catastrophe costs.
2. **Reinsurance Strategy:** Increase reinsurance limits specifically for Earthquake and Hurricane perils, as these represent the most significant tail-risk to the company's capital reserves.
3. **Operational Readiness:** Automate claim processing for high-frequency, low-severity events (like regional floods) to reduce administrative overhead during peak disaster seasons.
4. **Targeted Risk Mitigation:** Offer premium discounts to commercial policyholders who invest in risk mitigation (e.g., retrofitting for earthquakes, flood barriers) to lower overall claim severity.
